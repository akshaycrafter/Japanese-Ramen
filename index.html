07 FAQ

Quick answers, before you slurp.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins get the next open seat, usually within 5-10 minutes on weeknights. For weekends, tables of 4+, and the private room, book via WhatsApp the day before.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes. Our miso broth is plant-based. We also offer a vegan tantanmen with sesame chili broth and tofu. Ask your server about daily veggie specials.

Can I customise my bowl?

Absolutely. Extra chashu, soft-boiled egg, extra nori, less salt, extra spice, or swap the noodles for a thinner or thicker cut. Just tell your server how you want it.

Do you cater for gluten allergies?

We offer gluten-free rice noodles on request. Please mention it when you book or tell your server — we'll adjust the soy sauce and toppings to be gluten-free as well.

Is there an age limit?

All ages are welcome until 10pm. After 10pm, we switch to a quiet atmosphere — under-16s need a parent or guardian present. Kids' portions available on request for diners under 12.

What if I just want to try it out first?

Grab a single bowl at the counter for ₹250. If you love it (you will), come back for the Full Spread. No membership, no minimum, just good ramen.
